### Hi there 👋

I'm Rodrigo — a **Customer Engineer at Cloudflare**, where I help teams design and ship things on the edge.

Before that I spent years as a software engineer across a few different companies, building backends, mobile apps and web platforms. I started my career at **Cisco**, which is where the networking bug bit me — and it never really let go. Packets, protocols and the weird places latency hides are still my favourite rabbit hole.

A good chunk of those years was at a German software house. I owned the architecture and global rollout of an **OpenFGA** authorization layer for a live medical platform running in 21 countries — the kind of RBAC problem where getting a permission check wrong is not a rounding error. I also led a team of five through four milestones, all delivered on time, while still writing code myself. Roadmap on one screen, pull requests on the other.

Outside work I tinker with AI agent tooling, dotfiles I'll never stop tweaking, and small space-themed side projects.

### What I work with

**Edge & Cloudflare** — most of my time goes to the **Developer Platform**: Workers and isolates, Durable Objects, Workflows, Queues, D1 / R2 / KV / Vectorize, Workers AI and the Agents SDK — plus the DNS, TLS and delivery layer underneath it. I build and run live demos of all of it at [nobledemos.com/demo](https://nobledemos.com/demo), and wrote up how the platform fits together at [nobledemos.com/developer-platform](https://nobledemos.com/developer-platform).

**Networking** — TCP/IP, routing, BGP, DNS, TLS. The layer everything else quietly depends on.

**Agent tooling & AI** — TypeScript, Bun, OpenCode plugins, Biome, Vitest.

**Backend & full-stack** — Node.js, React, JavaScript, Java, Python, Shell, Docker, HTML/CSS.
